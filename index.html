    Using Home Planner

    Select furniture in the scene or the sidebar list. Edit its outer sizes in inches and its X/Z position in feet. R rotates; Delete removes; Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes; Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redoes.

    Save checkpoint remembers furniture and the current camera view. Reset to saved restores them; Undo can reverse the reset. Download project JSON to back up the complete floor plan and furniture.

    In Walk through, drag to look and use W A S D or arrow keys to move. Escape returns to the 3D home. Room buttons move you to a preset viewpoint.

    Murphy beds and recliners show open and closed endpoints. A dashed outline shows their deployed footprint. Moving paths, anchoring, occupant access, exact door sweeps, and delivery routes are not simulated.

    The scene is visual; the sidebar provides keyboard selection and numeric editing. This is not a fully nonvisual or touch-optimized CAD tool.

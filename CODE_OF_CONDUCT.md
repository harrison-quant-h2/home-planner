# Code of conduct

Home Planner welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people, and give constructive feedback. Respect other people's privacy and the ownership of their work.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexualized conduct, unwanted contact, and publication of private information are not acceptable in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants. Enforcement should be proportionate, consider context, and preserve the privacy of people involved.

To report conduct privately, contact a repository maintainer using a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available, use GitHub's **Report abuse** flow rather than publishing sensitive details. Do not use security advisories for conduct reports. Maintainers involved in an incident should not be its sole reviewer.
